## 0. The rules the document must follow (the sprint page, fetched Sat Oct 24, and docs/PDF-TEMPLATE.md)

- A research report as a PDF, in the official template's form: US Letter, 1 inch margins, single column, Old Standard
  TT 11 pt body, headings numbered 1 to 6 then Code and Data, Author Contributions, References, Appendix; title in
  capitals with footnote 1 "Research conducted at the AI Collusion Research Sprint, October 2026"; author block with
  "With / Apart Research".
- At most 8 pages excluding references and appendix; we aim for 4 to 5 pages of body.
- Abstract 150 to 250 words in the report. The submission form may cap at 150: keep a 150-word version ready.
- A required appendix titled exactly "Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations", and an LLM Usage Statement.
- Every figure and table numbered and captioned so the caption stands alone; each referenced in the text before it
  appears; text in figures legible at 100 percent.
- A source on every factual claim; one citation style throughout; the prior-work disclosure line in Methods or Code
  and Data; the pre-registration box with the real commit (824c801) and every prediction marked in Results.
- No em dashes anywhere. None of the generated-text tells in PDF-TEMPLATE.md section 6 (openers like "In recent
  years", "delve", "crucial", "robust", "notably", "furthermore", reflex triads, hedging stacks, "This" without a noun).
- Numbers in the body come from `notes/results-frozen.md` only, regenerated by `make freeze` at the start of the
  session and not touched afterwards.
- Judged on three dimensions, 1 to 5: Impact Potential and Innovation, Execution Quality, Presentation and Clarity.
