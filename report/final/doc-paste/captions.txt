Figure 1 (fig_quadrant_dollars.png). Each dot is one market. Horizontal: what buyers paid above the $90 competitive price over the whole market, in dollars (sum over trades). Vertical: share of the sellers' messages labelled collusive. Hollow dots are honest by design (no channel, or told the conduct is illegal); those above zero are the markets meant to be honest whose sellers coordinated anyway (Qwen told it is illegal, Haiku told not to discuss prices); crosses sent no message. Dashed lines mark the most expensive honest no-chat market of the two families with a price reference: for GPT-4.1 that honest market ($632) cost buyers more than any cartel. The chat monitor fires on every dot above zero; 16 of the 35 2025 cartels it flags sit left of zero.

Figure 2 (fig2_instructions.png). What the instruction sentences do, by seller model. Bars: share of messages labelled collusive. Dots: mean ask from round 6 (top axis, dollars); grey numbers: markets. "Set your ask independently" leaves coordination talk intact; adding "coordinating prices is illegal" ends Haiku's talk at no-chat prices, silences Sonnet, Opus and Luna, and is ignored by DeepSeek and Qwen; requiring an hourly market note beside it turns Sonnet's silence into honest talk; "do not discuss prices" produces no-undercut pacts without a number. Claude cells are 20-round samples plus the 30-round sets; Qwen faced algorithmic buyers (hollow dots).

Figure 3 (fig_gag.png). The 2025 overseer's gag, in rounds relative to the gag (all 20 watched markets were gagged between rounds 1 and 7). Left: share of seller messages labelled collusive. Middle: share of the sellers' private strategy notebooks that coordinate (hold a common price or avoid undercutting). Right: mean ask. The talk ends in both conditions; without CEO pressure the notebooks fade and asks fall toward the competitive price, with it the notebooks hold and asks stay near $96.5.

Figure A1 (fig_silent_pricing.png). The one silent high-pricing market as an illustration: Opus 5.5 under "set your ask independently" sent no message in 20 rounds and held its asks within about $0.10 of each other, against the range of the honest Haiku markets over the same rounds and the price screen's bar.

Figure A2 (fig_plan_message.png). Private plans and notebooks against public messages, 2025 chat markets. (a) Seller turns by condition. (b) By round, the share of turns whose private notebook coordinates and whose public message is collusive, under the overseer with and without CEO pressure.
