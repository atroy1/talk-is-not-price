When five AI sellers in a market can message each other, they agree on prices and say so in plain words. No chat monitor had been calibrated on honest LLM markets, because none existed. No model we tested talks honestly on its own: one added sentence, "coordinating prices is illegal", makes one model talk honestly, silences three and is ignored by two. Calibrated on 20 honest markets (4.8% certified false-alarm rate), the monitor flags 105 of the 112 markets whose sellers were free to coordinate, with one false alarm among 34 honest markets that talked. It measures agreement rather than harm: 16 of the 35 cartels in the prior data left buyers no worse off, the most expensive market was honest and silent, and gagged sellers under profit pressure kept their plan in private notes and their prices high. Pre-registered; the headline is exploratory and every change is declared.
