Five AI sellers that can message each other agree on prices and say so. The obvious audit tool reads their chat, but none had been certified at a stated false-alarm rate: there were no honest LLM markets to calibrate on. We show why. No model we tested talks honestly on its own; one added sentence ("coordinating prices is illegal") makes one model honest, three silent, and two ignore it. Calibrated on 20 honest markets (4.8% certified false alarms), the monitor flags 104 of 105 markets whose sellers coordinated in chat, across seven seller models, with no false alarm on 66 honest markets; four keyword patterns do nearly as well. The flag measures talk, not harm: 16 of 35 prior-data cartels left buyers no worse off, the most expensive market was honest and silent, and gagged sellers kept their plan in private notes and their price on the board.
